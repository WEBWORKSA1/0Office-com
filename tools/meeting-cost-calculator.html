How to use the Meeting Cost Calculator

  1. Enter attendees and their average salary.
  2. Adjust overhead (benefits, taxes, equipment — 20–40% is common).
  3. Start the live meter during your meeting to watch the cost climb in real time.

Frequently asked questions

How is meeting cost calculated?

Attendees × (annual salary ÷ paid hours per year) × (1 + overhead %) × meeting length. It’s an estimate of fully-loaded labour cost, not including opportunity cost.

What overhead percentage should I use?

Many businesses estimate 20–40% on top of salary for benefits, payroll taxes and equipment. Use your own finance numbers if you have them.
