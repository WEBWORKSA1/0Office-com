Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly to annual salary?

Hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. At 40 hours and 52 weeks, that’s hourly × 2,080.

Is the take-home figure exact?

No — it applies a single flat estimated tax rate. Real take-home depends on your country, brackets, deductions and benefits.
