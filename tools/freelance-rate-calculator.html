How to use the Freelance Rate Calculator

  1. Enter the income you want to take home and your monthly costs.
  2. Be honest about billable % — admin, sales and learning aren’t billable.
  3. Use the hourly rate as your floor, not your price.

Frequently asked questions

Why is billable percentage so important?

Freelancers rarely bill every working hour. At 65% billable on a 40-hour week, only 26 hours generate revenue.

Should I charge exactly this rate?

Treat it as your minimum. Price based on value and market rates for your niche, above this floor.
