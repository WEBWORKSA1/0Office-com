How to use the Free Invoice Generator

  1. Fill in your business and client details.
  2. Add line items — totals, discount and tax update live.
  3. Click Download / Print PDF and choose “Save as PDF”. Your draft auto-saves in this browser.

Frequently asked questions

Is this invoice generator really free?

Yes. No account, no watermark, no limits. Everything runs in your browser and your data is never uploaded.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Download / Print PDF, then choose “Save as PDF” as the printer. The page prints only the invoice, without site navigation or ads.

Is my invoice data stored?

Only in your own browser’s local storage so you can come back to a draft. Click “Start new” to clear it.
