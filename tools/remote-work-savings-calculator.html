Frequently asked questions

What costs does this include?

Commuting, office-day food, work clothing, extra care costs, minus added home costs. Time savings are valued at the hourly rate you enter.

How accurate is the employer figure?

It multiplies seats by your per-seat cost. Real savings depend on lease terms, utilities, equipment stipends and any coworking you still buy.
