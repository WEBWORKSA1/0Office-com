🔐 Strong Password Generator

Generate strong random passwords and passphrases locally with your browser's secure RNG. 100% free, private, runs in your browser.

How to use the Strong Password Generator

  1. Choose a length (16+ recommended) and character sets.
  2. Copy the password straight into your password manager.
  3. Nothing is sent over the network — generation uses crypto.getRandomValues().

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to generate passwords online?

This generator runs entirely in your browser using the Web Crypto API; nothing is transmitted or stored.

How long should a password be?

For most accounts, 16+ random characters or a 5-word passphrase, stored in a password manager.
